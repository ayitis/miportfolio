01 / The story

NUPTIAS is a visual exploration of love, intimacy and connection. Created for Impuribus, the editorial brings the brand's structured aesthetic into a softer, more emotional world.

Architectural silhouettes meet natural surroundings. Beyond familiar bridal imagery, the story celebrates quiet gestures, complicity and freedom.
