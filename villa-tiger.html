01 / The brand

A PRIVATE STAY,
DESIGNED AS A BRAND.

Villa Tiger is a private hospitality experience in Marrakech, transformed into a distinctive visual identity that brings together contemporary comfort, Moroccan warmth and an editorial approach to communication.

Brand Identity · Logo System · Art Direction · Social Media · Editorial Content

02 / Logo system

THE VISUAL IDENTITY

The arch evokes Marrakech’s architecture and the feeling of arrival. The letter T gives the brand a strong monogram, while the illustrated tiger brings character to its geometric frame.
